# Reports

A member can report another member: right-click them → **«Пожаловаться»** (Report) → describe what happened. Anyone except the owner can be reported. Until a report is handled, a second one about the same person can't be sent — the menu shows «Жалоба на рассмотрении» (Report under review).

## Where to look

When a new report arrives, a counter lights up on the shield button of the control panel. Control panel → **«Жалобы»** (Reports): tabs for new and reviewed reports.

## How to handle a report

The report card shows who reported whom and what they wrote. Then it depends on the situation:

- Take action right from the card — for example, ban the member.
- Or use the [member menu](/en/community/moderation#member-menu): hide the name, kick.
- Mark the report as reviewed — it moves to the «Рассмотренные» (Reviewed) tab, and the author can report this person again if needed.

## Who handles reports

- Reports about regular members — the owner and admins.
- Reports about admins — **only the owner**. Admins see such reports but can't handle them, including a report about themselves.

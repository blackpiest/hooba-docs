# Badges, titles, challenges

Each member has a mini-profile: a character, background, frame, up to 3 badges and a title. Members put it together themselves in the profile editor, and the owner decides what's available there.

## Badges and titles

- A **role badge** appears next to the nickname automatically — it's set up in the [role](/en/community/roles#settings).
- **Other badges and titles** are granted by the owner: right-click a member → **«Значки и титулы…»** (Badges and titles…). A grant takes effect immediately, and the member sees a «Новое» (New) mark in the editor.
- You can revoke them in the same place.

## Showcase

Control panel → **«Витрина»** (Showcase), and also the tabs «Значки» (Badges), «Титулы» (Titles), «Оформление» (Appearance):

- **Texts** of badges and titles — names and descriptions can be rewritten for your community.
- **Custom titles** — you can create new ones and delete them.
- **«Добавить» (Add) / «Убрать в запас» (Move to reserve)** — an item in reserve isn't visible in the editor to those who don't have it. This is how rare rewards are made: put a badge in reserve and grant it by hand or for a challenge.

The owner has access to everything in the editor.

## Challenges

A challenge is a condition that **automatically** gives a member a reward when met: a badge, title, frame, background or character.

Control panel → **«Испытания»** (Challenges) → new challenge:

1. Build the conditions. They can be combined ("and" / "or"):

   | Condition | Example |
   |---|---|
   | Messages | write 500 messages (in all or selected channels, for all time or over a period) |
   | Time in voice | spend 10 hours in voice channels |
   | Time in a game | play a game for N hours while in Hooba (games are picked from a list; several or any) |
   | Online time during certain hours | 5 hours online between 0:00 and 6:00 — a "Night owl" |
   | Active days | be active for 30 days |
   | Days in a row | log in 7 days in a row |
   | Invited people | invite 3 friends who registered |
   | Owns an item | already have a certain badge, frame, etc. |
   | Challenge completed | a chain: first one, then the next |

2. Choose a reward.
3. Publish.

Members see challenges and their progress on the «Испытания» (Challenges) tab of the profile editor. A challenge you no longer need goes to the archive.

Hours and days are counted in the community's time zone.

Game time is detected only by the desktop app on Windows: it isn't counted for members in a browser or on other systems. If the warning **«Нет в каталоге — время не считается»** (Not in the catalog — time isn't counted) appears under a condition, remove that game and pick it from the list again.

::: tip Activity tracking
The server counts activity with protection against farming:

- a message counts if it has at least 2 letters or digits, or an image ("+", ")))" don't count), and no more than once every 5 seconds;
- a minute in voice counts only if someone else is in the room and the member's sound isn't turned off — sitting alone or AFK doesn't count.
:::

# Announcements

An announcement is a window that pops up over the app for members at a scheduled time. It's good for important things: rules, events, maintenance. Only the owner can create announcements.

## Create one

Control panel → **«Объявления»** (Announcements) → new announcement:

1. Write the text.
2. Choose when to show it, by the community's clock.
3. Choose who sees it: **everyone**, members with certain **roles**, or specific **members**.

## How it is delivered

- At the scheduled time, the announcement goes to everyone who matches the chosen audience at that moment. Those online see it right away, others at their next login.
- Each person sees the announcement once.
- Anyone who joins the server after the scheduled time doesn't get it.

A scheduled announcement can be edited or deleted. Deletion is recorded in the log.
